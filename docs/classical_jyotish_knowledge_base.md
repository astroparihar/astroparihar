## 2. Divisional Charts (Varga) Mathematical Rules

*Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra (Chapter 6: Shodashvarga Adhyaya)* establishes that each division of a sign corresponds to an inner vibration of consciousness and destiny.

### 2.1 D1 Rashi Chart (Physical Manifestation)
- **Division:** $30^\circ 00'$ per house.
- **House System:** Equal Sign from Lagna (Sripathi/Parashari bhava chalit integration).

### 2.2 D9 Navamsha Chart (Spouse, Dharma & Inner Strength)
- **Division:** $3^\circ 20'$ ($200\text{ arcminutes}$) per Pada ($1/9\text{th}$ of a rashi).
- **Rule of Progression (BPHS Ch. 6, Slokas 15–18):**
  - **Fiery Signs (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius):** 1st Navamsha begins in Aries (Mesha) and runs consecutively.
  - **Earthy Signs (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn):** 1st Navamsha begins in Capricorn (Makara) and runs consecutively.
  - **Airy Signs (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius):** 1st Navamsha begins in Libra (Tula) and runs consecutively.
  - **Watery Signs (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces):** 1st Navamsha begins in Cancer (Karka) and runs consecutively.

### 2.3 D10 Dasamsa Chart (Career, Profession & Social Karma)
- **Division:** $3^\circ 00'$ ($180\text{ arcminutes}$) per division ($1/10\text{th}$ of a rashi).
- **Rule of Progression (BPHS Ch. 6, Slokas 19–21):**
  - **Odd Signs (Mesha, Mithuna, Simha, Tula, Dhanu, Kumbha):** Count begins from the sign itself.
  - **Even Signs (Vrishabha, Karka, Kanya, Vrishchika, Makara, Meena):** Count begins from the 9th sign from the occupied sign.

### 2.4 D7 Saptamsha Chart (Children & Creative Lineage)
- **Division:** $4^\circ 17' 08.57''$ ($257.14\text{ arcminutes}$) per division ($1/7\text{th}$ of a rashi).
- **Rule:** For odd signs, count begins from the sign itself; for even signs, count begins from the 7th sign.

## 3. Vimshottari Dasha Mathematical Derivation

*Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra (Chapter 46: Vimshottari Dasha Adhyaya)* identifies the 120-year cycle as the supreme predictive timeline (*Parama Ayus*) for the Kali Yuga.

### 3.1 Astronomical Constants & Nakshatra Lords
The 27 Nakshatras span $13^\circ 20' 00''$ ($800\text{ arcminutes}$) each. Their planetary lord sequence and years are:
| Lord | Nakshatras | Standard Span ($Y_M$) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ketu** | Ashwini, Magha, Mula | 7 Years |
| **Venus (Shukra)** | Bharani, Purva Phalguni, Purva Ashadha | 20 Years |
| **Sun (Surya)** | Krittika, Uttara Phalguni, Uttara Ashadha | 6 Years |
| **Moon (Chandra)** | Rohini, Hasta, Shravana | 10 Years |
| **Mars (Mangal)** | Mrigashira, Chitra, Dhanishta | 7 Years |
| **Rahu** | Ardra, Swati, Shatabhisha | 18 Years |
| **Jupiter (Guru)** | Punarvasu, Vishakha, Purva Bhadrapada | 16 Years |
| **Saturn (Shani)** | Pushya, Anuradha, Uttara Bhadrapada | 19 Years |
| **Mercury (Budha)** | Ashlesha, Jyeshtha, Revati | 17 Years |
| **Total** | | **120.00 Years** |

### 3.2 Birth Dasha Balance Formula
Let $\lambda_{\text{Moon}}$ be the sidereal longitude of the Moon, and $\lambda_{\text{start}}$ be the starting longitude of the birth nakshatra:
$$\text{Arc}_{\text{traversed}} = \lambda_{\text{Moon}} - \lambda_{\text{start}}$$
$$\text{Arc}_{\text{remaining}} = 13^\circ 20' - \text{Arc}_{\text{traversed}} = 800' - \text{Arc}_{\text{traversed}}$$
$$\text{Balance Years} = \left(\frac{\text{Arc}_{\text{remaining}}}{800'}\right) \times Y_M$$

### 3.3 Antardasha & Pratyantardasha Formulas
- **Antardasha Span:** $\text{Duration} = \frac{Y_M \times Y_A}{120}\text{ years}$
- **Pratyantardasha Span:** $\text{Duration} = \frac{Y_A \times Y_P}{120}\text{ years} = \frac{Y_M \times Y_A \times Y_P}{14400}\text{ years}$

## 4. Classical Yogas Algorithmic Definitions

### 4.1 Pancha Mahapurusha Yogas (BPHS Ch. 75)
Formed when a non-luminary planet is placed in a **Kendra (1st, 4th, 7th, 10th House)** in its **own sign (*Swakshetra*)** or **exaltation sign (*Uccha*)**:
1. **Ruchaka Yoga (Mars):** Aries, Scorpio, or Capricorn in Kendra.
2. **Bhadra Yoga (Mercury):** Gemini or Virgo in Kendra.
3. **Hamsa Yoga (Jupiter):** Sagittarius, Pisces, or Cancer in Kendra.
4. **Malavya Yoga (Venus):** Taurus, Libra, or Pisces in Kendra. *(Present in Audit Benchmark Native)*
5. **Sasa Yoga (Saturn):** Capricorn, Aquarius, or Libra in Kendra.

### 4.2 Kendra-Trikona Raja Yogas (BPHS Ch. 34–41)
- **Kendra Houses (1, 4, 7, 10):** Pillars of Vishnu (Action, Manifestation).
- **Trikona Houses (1, 5, 9):** Shrines of Lakshmi (Grace, Fortune, Dharma).
- **Raja Yoga Rule:** The mutual conjunction, aspect, or mutual sign exchange (*Parivartana*) of a Kendra lord with a Trikona lord generates an auspicious Raja Yoga, elevating social influence and karmic standing.

### 4.3 Viparita Raja Yogas (Uttara Kalamrita 4.22)
Occurs when lords of dusthanas (6th, 8th, 12th) occupy dusthana houses without association from benefic Kendra/Trikona lords:
- **Harsha Yoga:** 6th lord in 6th, 8th, or 12th.
- **Sarala Yoga:** 8th lord in 6th, 8th, or 12th.
- **Vimala Yoga:** 12th lord in 6th, 8th, or 12th.

## 5. Classical Doshas & Algorithmic Rules

### 5.1 Mangal Dosha (Kuja Dosha)
- **Primary Rule (*BPHS* Ch. 81 / *Brihat Jataka*):** Mars placed in the **1st, 4th, 7th, 8th, or 12th house** from the Ascendant (or Moon).
- **Canonical Cancellation Exceptions (*Bhanga*):**
  1. Mars placed in its own sign (Aries/Scorpio) or exaltation sign (Capricorn).
  2. Conjunction with Jupiter or aspect by Jupiter.
  3. Both marital partners are Manglik (mutual cancellation).

### 5.2 Kaal Sarp Dosha & The 12 Classical Types
Kaal Sarp Dosha occurs when all seven classical grahas (Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn) are hemmed completely between the Rahu–Ketu nodal axis ($180^\circ$ hemisphere).

The 12 canonical variations are determined by **Rahu's house position from Lagna**:
1. **Ananta:** Rahu in 1st, Ketu in 7th
2. **Kulika:** Rahu in 2nd, Ketu in 8th
3. **Vasuki:** Rahu in 3rd, Ketu in 9th
4. **Shankhapala:** Rahu in 4th, Ketu in 10th
5. **Padma:** Rahu in 5th, Ketu in 11th
6. **Mahapadma:** Rahu in 6th, Ketu in 12th
7. **Takshaka:** Rahu in 7th, Ketu in 1st
8. **Karkotaka:** Rahu in 8th, Ketu in 2nd
9. **Shankhanaad:** Rahu in 9th, Ketu in 3rd
10. **Ghataka:** Rahu in 10th, Ketu in 4th
11. **Vishdhar (Active in Benchmark):** **Rahu in 11th House, Ketu in 5th House.** Indicates intense financial striving, elder sibling friction, and high ambition resolved through continuous Shiva worship and Naga parihars.
12. **Sheshnaag:** Rahu in 12th, Ketu in 6th
